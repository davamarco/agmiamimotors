The Lamborghini Huracan is not merely a car - it is a philosophy made kinetic. Powered by a naturally aspirated 5.2-litre V10 producing 640 horsepower at 8,000 rpm, it delivers a sensory experience that no turbocharged rival can replicate. The sound alone - that operatic wail rising to a crescendo at full throttle - is worth the price of admission.

Behind the wheel in Miami, the Huracan transforms Ocean Drive into your personal proving ground. Four-wheel steering and torque vectoring mean the car moves like a living extension of your intentions, attacking every corner with surgical precision.

Our Miami-maintained example presents in pristine condition, fully detailed before each rental. Your experience begins the moment our white-glove team delivers the car to your location. No line. No waiting. Just keys in hand and 640 horses beneath your right foot.

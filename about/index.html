About · The Founder

Built by someone who actually loves these cars.

I didn’t start AG Motors because I saw a gap in the market. I started it because I’ve always been obsessed with these cars — the sound, the engineering, and the feeling of driving something built without compromise.

Miami is one of those places where that obsession makes sense every single day. So I turned it into a business.

Every car we offer is there for a reason.

And every price is structured the way I’d want to see it if I were the one renting — no hidden fees, no last-minute surprises, and no fine print designed to catch you out.

When you book with AG Motors, you’re not dealing with a call center or a faceless rental company. You’re dealing directly with people who genuinely care about the cars, the service, and making your time in Miami unforgettable.

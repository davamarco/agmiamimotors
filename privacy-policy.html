Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

Information We Collect

When you submit a booking request through our website, we collect the information you provide directly through the booking form on agmotorsmiami.com — your name, phone number, email address (optional), rental dates, and vehicle preferences.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to process your booking request, to contact you and confirm availability and rental details via WhatsApp, phone, or email, and to improve the quality of our service. We do not use your information for any purpose beyond fulfilling and supporting your rental request.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties. The only exception is where it's necessary to provide our service — for example, using the phone number you provide to contact you through WhatsApp regarding your reservation.

Cookies & Analytics

Our website uses Google Analytics and Google Ads to understand website traffic and measure the performance of our advertising. These services may collect anonymous data about your visit — such as pages viewed and general location — through cookies and similar technologies. This data does not personally identify you.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you provide to us from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

Your Rights
